# Background study — review requirements

User feedback, September 11, 2026:

- The announcement is the strongest social direction because it has atmosphere and visual interest.
- Its repeated logo and extra footer text are too much. Use one logo and keep the composition focused.
- The insight is hard to read and boring. The quote resembles a corporate letter. Social should feel cultural.
- Explore backgrounds as a separate reusable layer beneath crisp graphics and components.
- Include soft-focus photographic scenery, especially a blurrier field, and abstract graphic art in Recoup colors.
- Compare different styles and examples before approving a direction. All new backgrounds remain proposed.

Updated scope: **50 images across 10 styles**, with five selected variations per style. Twenty-five photographic studies and twenty-five abstract graphic-art studies. The shared 22-color world supplies selected color combinations, alongside reference-only and open-color variations. Any extra generations already in progress are kept outside the main review collection. Numeric colors guide generation; they do not guarantee pixel-exact matching.

## Deliverable criteria

- Individually downloadable landscape images, with no text, logos, or baked-in components.
- Photographic options should use intentional optical defocus, grain, and recognizable natural light.
- Abstract options should vary their medium and composition, not only change a gradient color.
- Explore blue, forest, lime, pale blue, and white in different proportions.
- Leave usable areas for a headline, speaker frame, or card. A background should still have a point of view when seen on its own.
- Review by photographic/abstract family and style in the visual gallery. Keep prompts with each generated source.
- Keep this study in labs until visual approval; do not migrate it to the marketing repo yet.
