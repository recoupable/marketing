# The Recoup Podcast — three kit options

Proposed designs for review. Episode details, speaker names, and the quote are examples; no guest footage or soundtrack is included.

## The three directions

- **Cut & shift:** blue folded planes, lime seams, and asymmetrical type. The opening uses large intersecting shapes; the conversation frame uses a quieter edge treatment.
- **Repeat & reveal:** forest tonal repeats with lime lettering. Light editorial panels interrupt the pattern on the show cover, thumbnail, and quote card. The speaker layouts use small pattern accents.
- **Open & overlap:** lime layers and broad crops with forest type. Layering carries the opening and cover; blue details provide contrast around conversation footage.

Each direction includes both smaller and larger pattern treatments. Pattern scale follows the format and the role of the text; a single fixed amount of decoration is not applied to every asset.

## Files in each kit

- Show cover: 3000 × 3000 PNG and editable SVG.
- Episode thumbnail, opening still, end card, and clean background: 1920 × 1080 PNG and SVG.
- One- and two-speaker frames: 1920 × 1080 PNG and SVG with transparent camera windows.
- Vertical clip frame: 1080 × 1920 PNG and SVG with a transparent camera window.
- Quote card: 1080 × 1350 PNG and SVG.
- Separate transparent labels for one speaker, two speakers, and vertical clips.
- Camera layout previews ending in `-preview.png`. These contain placeholder windows for review only.
- Silent 6-second opening MP4 and 12-second looping background MP4, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps.

## Use in a video editor

For a static frame, place the speaker footage beneath the solo, duo, or vertical PNG. The transparent window reveals the footage underneath. Crop footage to fill the window.

For a moving background, place the clean background MP4 on the bottom track, camera footage above it, and the matching `-labels` PNG at the top. Repeat the background clip for the length of the conversation. For vertical edits, crop or resize the background to the vertical canvas before placing the camera and labels.

Camera windows in pixels:

- Solo: x 64, y 154, width 1792, height 814.
- Duo: x 64 / 980, y 210, width 876, height 690 each.
- Vertical: x 52, y 326, width 976, height 1216.

The interactive page allows editing the example title, episode number, names, and quote before downloading a PNG or SVG. Sample kits in the ZIP retain their example text. The opening and background MP4s contain no episode-specific text. The show title is The Recoup Podcast.

DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono are embedded in the editable SVGs, with font files and licenses included. Episode headlines retain the preferred DM Sans 450 weight and a 110 px maximum at 1920 × 1080. Long headlines fit their allotted space; square and portrait formats use proportional type sizes.

## Motion and reuse

Openings introduce the symbol, reveal the show name, and hold. Pattern motion affects selected faces or shapes slowly over a 12-second cycle. Conversation framing and text remain stationary.

Use the native scene and pattern modules for consistent updates to all exports. To extend a kit, choose the format first, choose small/medium/large coverage based on its reading area, then vary crop, repetition, and selective color within the chosen direction. Keep the actual Recoup mark intact. Avoid literal music props, thin ornamental contour lines, or identical corner gradients across the set.
