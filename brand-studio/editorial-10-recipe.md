# Recoup editorial pattern study

Six proposed directions for visual review. No pattern family in this study is approved yet.

## Keep

- Headline-first composition, actual DM Sans at a restrained weight, and ample quiet space.
- Roughly 80% one dominant color. Use blue, forest, pale blue, white, or lime as the field.
- Recoup's own offset blocks, curved joins, and overlapping panels as starting geometry.
- Blue #007EBD, deep blue #0565BB, lime #D6FF62, forest #132B26, ink #152E37, pale #F0F7FA, white #FFFFFF. Cyan may connect the blues.
- Export the artwork and headline separately. Keep the real Recoup mark exact when it is used.

## Change deliberately

Choose the pattern before choosing its color treatment. Match the crop, scale, and title position to the article and the surrounding covers. A gradient can describe a face of a shape, travel through a cutout, distinguish layers, spread along a whole edge, or live within a soft impression. A composition can also remain entirely flat.

There is no universal bottom-right glow, percentage, or corner position. Keep the headline area quiet. Review neighboring covers together; vary at least two of shape family, crop, dominant color, or title placement when adjacent covers feel repetitive.

## Six hypotheses

1. **Offset blocks:** broad filled fragments with stepped levels and curved joins; directional light within a face.
2. **Cutout join:** one oversized asymmetric form with deliberate negative space; color travels through the form.
3. **Layered panels:** generous rounded surfaces cropped into the frame; tonal changes distinguish overlaps.
4. **Interwoven bands:** filled bands and clean over-under gaps; a completely flat color treatment.
5. **Stepped ribbons:** broad stepped forms along one edge; a restrained horizontal wash across the base.
6. **Soft imprint:** one defocused crop of the exact mark; localized light and an even field for the headline.

## Reusable art-direction brief

Create a Recoup editorial cover for [article title]. The article's central idea is [one sentence]. Use [one proposed or approved pattern family] with [dominant color] occupying approximately 80% of the composition. Place the artwork [position and crop] and reserve quiet space [headline position]. Use [flat color / a specific light treatment] because [composition reason]. Keep the headline readable at blog-grid size. Use Recoup's palette and actual DM Sans. Preserve exact vector brand assets. Previous neighboring covers used [families, crops, palettes, and light placement]; vary [two choices] so this cover belongs to the same brand without repeating its neighbors. Deliver a cover and a text-free artwork layer, with editable vector type. Label the result proposed until visually reviewed.

Avoid copied LangChain dot fields, converging terminal lines, thin corner contour waves, inscrutable symbols, literal music equipment, photoreal objects, unnecessary badges, and repeated logo stamps. Metaphorical and anthropomorphic illustrations remain valid for article interiors when they explain an idea better than a chart or diagram; this study does not settle that illustration style.

## Review views

- **Same headline:** compare patterns with the copy and type size held constant.
- **Example articles:** inspect the options with different representative titles.
- **Patterns only:** inspect the artwork independently of typography.

The native SVG files use editable text and embedded DM Sans. PNGs outline the same local font for reliable rendering. These assets contain no generative-image approximation of the typography or logo.
