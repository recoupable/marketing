## Reusable prompt

Create one landscape 16:9 blog thumbnail for Recoup, for music executives, music funds, and rightsholders.

Article: [ARTICLE TITLE]
Main point: [ONE CLEAR ARGUMENT]
Exact short headline, including line breaks: [HEADLINE]
Visual idea: [ONE CONCEPT THAT SUPPORTS THE ARTICLE]
Type treatment: [BOLD SENTENCE CASE / LIGHT EDITORIAL / CONDENSED CAPITALS / MIXED-SIZE HEADLINE / HIGHLIGHTED PHRASE / MONOSPACED HEADLINE]
Composition: [TYPE AREA] and [ILLUSTRATION AREA]
Recent covers: [THE LAST SIX SUBJECTS, TYPE TREATMENTS, LAYOUTS, AND PALETTES]

Use the selected source-review cover as a reference for the illustration language and background texture. Create a new subject and arrangement. Draw simple ivory paper-like shapes with crisp, subtly imperfect forest outlines and a few purposeful lime accents. Use thin connectors only when they explain a relationship. Keep each idea readable at thumbnail size. No enclosing panel, photorealism, glossy 3D objects, robot imagery, logos, watermarks, or extra words.

Background: a full-bleed finely grainy, softly diffused gradient. Choose blue #007EBD / #168DDD into lime #D6FF62, OR deep forest #132B26 into blue #168DDD. Vary the dominant color, transition, and bloom position while preserving the palette. Keep the background quiet and sufficiently dark behind ivory text. Put forest text on lime highlights. Keep all text and meaningful details inside a 6% safe margin, with clear separation between headline and illustration.

Render the exact headline prominently in the chosen treatment, using contemporary sans-serif typography or the selected monospaced treatment. Verify spelling, punctuation, line breaks, and contrast. Across the series, alternate text treatments and composition as well as the visual idea. Do not merely swap headlines over the same illustration. Use real source material for factual diagrams; these conceptual illustrations must not imply fabricated results or data.
