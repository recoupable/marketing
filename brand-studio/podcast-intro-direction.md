# Recoup Podcast · Opening direction

A council of three specialist subagents reviewed the brief: a motion design director, a logo animation specialist, and an art/brand director. These are design judgments, not audience research.

## Ideas considered

| Direction | Idea | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connection pass | Opposing blue planes reveal the mark. The large symbol settles into place and introduces the name. | Selected: makes Recoup’s distinctive bend the memorable event. |
| Light finds the mark | A broad cyan light reveals the silhouette, then continues into the lettering. | Quieter and more atmospheric; less decisive as a recurring show signature. |
| Through the mark | Start inside an enormous symbol-shaped opening and pull back into the complete identity. | A more cinematic campaign treatment; more visual activity than the podcast needs. |

## Final choreography

- 0.08–0.95s: opposed reveals expose the lower and upper ends of the symbol, with a four-frame offset. Its exact geometry remains fixed.
- 0.95–1.28s: a short recognition hold on the large, complete symbol.
- 1.28–2.48s: the symbol contracts and travels left in one continuous movement.
- 2.22–3.28s: Recoup and Podcast reveal in sequence after the symbol clears their space.
- 3.55–5.50s: the approved title card holds still for a clean edit.

## Craft decisions

Keep the exact icon path, optical lift, spacing, white identity, and600 / 300 type weights. No bounce, spin, particle effects, or per-letter gymnastics. The symbol reveal is the main event; the background supports it. A reviewer caught a hairline seam during the recognition hold, so the mask layers switch to the complete icon as soon as the reveal finishes.

The opening works silently. Sound design can be added later without making the visual timing depend on it.
