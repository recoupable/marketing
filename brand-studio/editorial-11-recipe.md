# Recoup pattern refinements

The user liked all six round10 directions. This study refines those six, keeps their original article titles, and preserves the originals for comparison. The refined versions remain proposed until visually reviewed.

## The refinement pass

- **Offset blocks:** keep the quiet blue field and stepped composition; add fine texture only within the lit block.
- **Cutout join:** soften the diagonal highlight into a broad transition inside the blue form.
- **Layered panels:** remove the lime dash, align the corner treatment, and clean up the blue layers.
- **Interwoven bands:** preserve flat white and flat colored bands; normalize bends and crossing gaps.
- **Stepped ribbons:** replace the muddy olive layer with a cleaner cool mint and quiet the bottom wash.
- **Soft imprint:** let a clearer upper edge fade into softer diffusion; give the title more breathing room.

## Shared visual rules

Keep actual DM Sans at weight 450, generous negative space, and approximately 80% one dominant color. Preserve the Recoup mark exactly when using it. Extend its relationship between straight runs and curved joins; avoid a wallpaper of repeated logos.

Within each motif, use consistent bend and gap dimensions. Change crop, scale, layer count, and placement to fit the composition. Do not force every kind of shape to use the same pixel radius: the proportions of a broad block and a narrow ribbon differ.

Use color and finish to clarify the construction. Light can belong to a shape's face, a cutout, a layered surface, or a blurred imprint. Grain stays within selected artwork and off the type. Keep some covers fully flat and crisp. Do not apply a repeated corner glow to the set.

Original reference colors: blue #007EBD, deep blue #0565BB, lime #D6FF62, forest #132B26, ink #152E37, pale #F0F7FA, white #FFFFFF. Restrained cyan and cool mint are supporting colors in this study, not replacements for the dominant palette.

## Choose a visual for an article

The pattern's association should support the article without needing a literal icon. Layering can suggest accumulated context; interweaving can suggest collaboration; steps can suggest progression or a repeatable process. The soft imprint can support a broader strategy article. These are starting associations, not fixed categories that every article must follow.

Read neighboring covers together. Alternate light and dark, sharp and soft, close crops and open fields. Preserve type family and quiet title placement. For a long headline, move or reflow the block before shrinking it. The soft-imprint refinement uses a minimum 128px right safe area at 1920px export width.

## Reusable brief

Refine a Recoup cover for [article title] using [chosen pattern family]. Preserve [the approved composition, palette, and type]. Improve only [specific detail]. The pattern should gently support [central idea]. Use [flat / selectively textured / diffused] artwork, with light placed [where and why]. Keep the title area quiet. Review at 360px width beside [neighboring covers], then inspect the larger artwork. Preserve the original for comparison. Export a 1920×1080 PNG and editable SVG, plus the artwork without text. Mark the result proposed for visual review.

The review page includes **Before & after** and **Blog grid** views. At full desktop width, comparison images are capped at 360px; the blog grid uses the same cap and reflows on smaller screens. Compare the visible results before promoting a refinement.

Article-interior diagrams, charts, infographics, and purposeful metaphorical or anthropomorphic illustrations remain a separate design task. This refinement does not settle that illustration style.
