# Recoup Podcast — Atmospheric studies

Original background explorations with the same single-line identity:
Recoup symbol + Recoup Podcast. No “The” and no subtitle.

## Identity

Recoup uses the website's DM Sans Variable font at weight 600, with wordmark
tracking scaled from −1.1px at 28px. Podcast uses DM Sans 300. At 1920×1080,
the type is 96px and the symbol 100px tall; the complete row is centered.
The symbol uses the exact website PageMark geometry. It is never AI-drawn.

## Background approach

The backgrounds were created using the built-in image-generation tool.
They explore flowing bands, refraction, elliptical apertures, woven forms,
soft contours, and optical interference. All use Recoup's palette as the
starting point, with fine grain and varied focus. Large forms continue
across the canvas; a quieter center comes from contrast and focus.
They are original abstractions, not reproductions of the SI artwork.

The seventh study, **Painted sky** (`sky`), uses the homepage image at
`public/images/sky/hero-sky.webp` as a color and atmosphere reference.
Editorial blue and painterly clouds replace photographic detail, with a
white identity over the calmer blue center.

The eighth study, **Cloud print** (`sky-print`), simplifies the sky into a
cobalt field and an oversized cloud silhouette entering from the lower right.
Limited tones and powdery edges give it a printed illustration treatment.
The white identity is unchanged for a direct comparison with Painted sky.

**Daylight folds** (`daylight`) and **Blue exposure** (`blue-exposure`) draw
on the homepage hero's blue, luminosity, and openness through original abstract
compositions. Translucent planes and a broad sweep of light replace recognizable
clouds. Both retain the same white identity for comparison. They were generated
from text briefs without an image reference to avoid repeating the sky imagery.

The exact generation prompts are included in generated-prompts-a.md,
generated-prompts-b.md, generated-prompts-sky.md, generated-prompts-sky-print.md,
and generated-prompts-blue-abstractions.md. These studies are
proposals for visual review.

## Glass studies

**Glass / Original** (`glass-original`) uses the selected reference image
`assets/backgrounds/bg-05-01.png` unchanged beneath the shared podcast identity.
The icon and lettering invert the exact background colors beneath their shapes
using an sRGB channel inversion and vector mask. The effect is embedded in both
the PNG and editable SVG, with the source background unchanged.

**Glass / Blue dominant** (`glass-blue`) and **Glass / Lime light** (`glass-lime`)
are image-generated variations that preserve the original's tactile glass
and diffused light. The blue version uses white lettering; the lime version uses
deep forest. All three share the same icon geometry, type weights, and placement.
Prompts and source provenance are recorded in `generated-prompts-glass.md`.

## Refined favorite

The user selected **Daylight folds** from the screenshot as the preferred
composition. **Daylight folds / Refined** (`daylight-refined`) preserves that
structure, with slightly deeper blue and a hint of cloud-like diffusion within
the existing edge lights. Following review, green was removed from the background
and the icon and lettering now use exact Recoup lime (#D6FF62).
The original remains available for comparison. The edit prompt is included in
`generated-prompts-daylight-refined.md`.

## Exports

The named PNGs are 1920×1080 title cards. The -background PNGs are original
generated artwork at their native sizes. SVGs contain embedded raster
backgrounds and embedded local fonts, with editable type and vector logo.
They are self-contained but the background is not editable vector geometry.
All-options.png is a contact sheet. Fonts and licenses are included.
These are static cover/intro-frame studies; no new motion or audio is included.
