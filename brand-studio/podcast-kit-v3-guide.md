# The Recoup Podcast — Color and corner studies

18 title cards: six palettes × three pattern treatments. Each is 1920 × 1080.

The selected composition stays fixed: centered symbol, single-line show name,
and the subtitle “Conversations on AI and music.”

## Identity

The website wordmark is DM Sans Variable at weight 600. Its tracking is
−1.1 px at 28 px, scaled proportionally to −4.832143 px at this 123 px size.
“The” and “Podcast” use the same font at weight 300. The symbol is the exact
website PageMark. The subtitle uses weight 350.

## Reusable composition recipe

Keep the symbol at the horizontal center, y=212, 141 px tall. Set the show
name on baseline y=570 at 123 px, with the Recoup wordmark at 600 and the
surrounding words at 300. Center the complete line, allowing 34.44 px between
the three words. Center the 38 px subtitle on baseline y=655.

Choose one base color and a high-contrast title color from the Recoup palette.
Use two close tonal colors for the pattern. Group repeated shapes near three
or four corners, crop some beyond the frame, and vary their size and angle.
Leave the center open through placement of the shapes themselves. Confine
any gradient to one or two small pattern pieces; do not wash the text field
with a repeated corner glow. Keep the title layout identical across studies.

Split links use paired rounded forms. Turning petals use rotated fans.
Looped lines use broad open outlines. These are alternatives to compare,
not three patterns to combine in one card.

## Files

PNG files are ready to use. SVG files contain editable text and embedded
local fonts. The fonts and licenses are included. The all-options PNG is a
review contact sheet. These are static title/background studies; earlier
