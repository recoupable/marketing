As of October 11, 2026 UTC, the production Songstats key is configured. One public
artist search using the local API environment credential returned 200 in 448ms.
This verifies that lookup and credential only; it does not prove production secret
parity, broader coverage, history depth, quota, reliability or contract cost.

Retain the existing integration during evaluation. No route is retired by this
reconciliation, and no blanket promise of restored public coverage is made.
Expansion, broad route re-publication and recurring provider fan-out stay pending
quota/cost confirmation through [mono#325](https://github.com/recoupable/mono/issues/325),
[App#1987](https://github.com/recoupable/app/issues/1987) and
[API#875](https://github.com/recoupable/api/pull/875). These are tracking links,
not proof the implementations are delivered.

## Current track statistics

Supply exactly one of `isrc`, `songstats_track_id`, `spotify_track_id`,
`apple_music_track_id`, plus a required `source`. For Spotify with an ISRC, the
measurement store is preferred when it has data; otherwise the API falls back to
Songstats. Other requested sources use Songstats. Preserve `data_source` when
returned; an unlabeled provider payload is not evidence of an Apify measurement.
Unavailable/429 responses must be surfaced, without an unbounded retry loop or
invented metrics. Recoup balance limits are separate from provider quota.

## Evaluation still required

A current private provider proposal offers usage-based billing. Activation of
that proposal, the configured key agreement, unique-request counting and the
relationship to Recoup research charges remain unverified. Confirm those terms
and monthly/daily/request quotas before expanding polling. Then sample
large and small artists across Spotify, Apple, TikTok, YouTube and playlist/radio
coverage; verify earliest and latest historic dates against dated samples;
measure repeated success, latency and 429/Retry-After behavior; inspect dependent
measurement jobs before choosing caching TTLs or changing their frequency.

A single passing lookup is insufficient to set a caching policy or claim uptime.
Record sample dates, exact operations, provenance, units consumed and errors;
keep commercial contract details in their approved private home.
