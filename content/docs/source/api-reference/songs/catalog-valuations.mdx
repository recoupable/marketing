---
title: 'Valuation history availability'
description: 'Saved valuation history is unfinished; current estimates do not establish a history.'
---

Saved catalog valuation history is not available through
`GET /api/catalogs/{catalogId}/valuations`. Current valuation calculations and
catalog measurements do not establish that estimates were saved or can be read
back as a dated series.

## Existing implementation work

[Reconciliation #326](https://github.com/recoupable/mono/issues/326) owns this
outcome through [App #1911](https://github.com/recoupable/app/issues/1911) and
[API #795](https://github.com/recoupable/api/pull/795).
[API #1059](https://github.com/recoupable/api/pull/1059) continues that work as a
held draft of internal adapters. It does not register a REST route, MCP tool or
production caller and does not connect successful valuation runs to persistence.

The existing owner must finish the access prerequisite, run-to-save integration,
public read contract and authorized deployed save/readback before this feature
can be advertised. Removing the unfinished operation from the callable
specification does not complete or cancel valuation history. Do not rerun a paid
valuation solely to try to recover an unconfirmed save.
