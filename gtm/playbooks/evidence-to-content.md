# Turn evidence into useful content

Adapted 2026-09-14 from consulting's sourced-insight and post-first workflow. Its historical workers are not deployed by this migration.

## Capture one useful observation

Create a small record with an ID, a specific observation, who it matters to, a source link, a quote or precise evidence reference, the observation date, your interpretation, and its status. Use public research for public drafts. Keep private call evidence in private storage unless its disclosure is separately approved.

For Music Moneyball, begin with the [principles](../research/music-rights/reference/principles.md), then read the relevant [guest note](../research/music-rights/podcast/guests/_index.md) and transcript. The principle is a synthesis; the interview is the evidence. Check attribution and important quotations against the source recording before publication.

Separate what the speaker said, your interpretation, and the Recoup point of view. A guest describing a problem does not endorse Recoup or prove that every company has the same problem.

## Make a brief, then a complete post

The brief should answer: who is this for, what situation will they recognize, what does the evidence show, what useful lesson follows, and what response would be valuable?

Write a native LinkedIn post that teaches something on its own. Start with the concrete problem or observation. Explain the useful point in plain language. Use only supported examples and a relevant next action. Do not require a reader to open an unwritten article to understand the post.

Review it as the buyer: is it recognizable, useful, and credible? Then edit for clarity. Remove generic claims, unsupported results, borrowed personal stories, and language that confuses an experiment with an available product. Use Recoup's current brand guidance without importing Sidney's personal account identity.

For product content, inspect the released feature and what a user can now do. Combine related changes into a useful story. A code change alone is not proof of adoption or customer benefit.

## Review, publish, learn

Keep the source reference, brief, draft, review status, reviewer, approval for the exact version, intended channel, and intended sender together. Editing approved copy should require review of the new version. A draft is not a publication.

After an authorized publication, save the returned public URL and actual publication time. Connect relevant responses and resulting conversations to that item in private records. Record whether a buyer explicitly mentioned the content, whether the source is merely a tracking link, or whether the connection is unknown. Do not claim causation from timing alone.

Expand into an article, email, or other format when the idea has useful response or a clear editorial reason. Count meaningful conversations and resulting opportunities alongside content activity. Publishing volume alone does not establish demand.

The approval queue and response-to-opportunity tracking are items to build in [BUILD_PLAN.md](../BUILD_PLAN.md), not capabilities supplied by this playbook.
