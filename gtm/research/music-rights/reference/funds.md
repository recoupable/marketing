## How to use this

1. Pick the **vehicle** first (public co, PE fund, insurer, credit, company).
2. Pick the **layer** (icons, producer royalties, film/TV, leakage, AI).
3. Open the guest transcript before asserting their strategy.
4. For Recoup podcast booking, skip anyone whose thesis is already on this
   table unless you have a *new* machine to inspect.
