# Research instructions

Read [README.md](README.md) before using counts or audience files. This is a dated research snapshot, not live CRM state or a campaign result.

- The company evidence CSV owns qualification evidence and review status; the six audience lists are derived outputs. Exclude held rows.
- Preserve primary source URLs, source quotes, dates, and relationship notes. Recheck a role or acquisition before asserting it is current.
- Podcast guest identity comes from the RSS-derived index. Machine transcripts can mishear names and words; verify important claims and quotes with the original audio. A principle is an interpretation unless directly supported by its cited passage.
- The selected executive list contains public role evidence, not inferred personal addresses. Keep future personal/contact exports out of this public folder.
- Count employer records, unique buyers, and matched members separately. Only a live Campaign Manager result can supply matched reach.
- Keep new research here and reusable software under `gtm/engine`; the audience builder remains beside this dataset for reproducibility.
- The import manifest is historical provenance. Preserve it as the initial receipt and use Git history for later edits. Do not rewrite the old source hash to disguise a changed source.
- Do not run audio ingestion/transcription to read the included corpus; its text is already present. Generated audio and processing files stay ignored.
