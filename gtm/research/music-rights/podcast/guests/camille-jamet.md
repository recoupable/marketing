## Machine

Catalog **streaming growth**, not acquisition. Pick tracks by **engagement** — "the save rate and the track per listener" `[04:18]–[04:26]` — not by biggest current streamer. Biggest track "isn't necessarily going to get you the biggest return on your effort" `[06:07]`.

Flywheel `[18:34]+`: content → audience → marketing (playlisting etc.) → read signals → put weight behind what works → repeat at larger scale. Hone in on one platform with "the biggest audience the biggest and the best tools," test, then scale `[12:51]–[12:56]`.
