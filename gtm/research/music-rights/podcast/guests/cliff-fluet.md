## Machine

Not a fund. The lawyer who tells financial buyers they are **not buying royalty statements**.

Ep 15 `[05:53]`:

> "you're not buying royalty statements you're buying underlying rights"

And `[06:23]`: if you don't know the difference "you really are negotiating blind."

Legal risk "doesn't decay income it can destroy it" — a title dispute is not a curve (ep 15 `[07:08]` / `[08:17]`). Diligence looks at gaps, consents, reversions, claims, unregistered neighbouring rights `[10:41]–[10:49]`. Fixing an approval chain can *unlock* a stream `[10:59]`.

Precision ask for sellers `[15:24]–[15:34]`: "what are you selling is it underlying rights is it masters is it publishing… is it right to share or is it subject reversion… get precise write it."

## Repeatable process (ep 16)
