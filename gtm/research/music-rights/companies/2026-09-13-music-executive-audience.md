## Executive targeting and activation

1. Start with the **priority company list**, worldwide targetable locations, and seniority
   **Owner OR Partner OR CXO OR VP**, subject to live category availability. If reach is too small,
   pool the core segments before widening seniority.
2. Keep operators/advisers and institutional capital in separate tests. Identify actual music
   practices/teams before use. Shamrock and HarbourView also invest beyond music, so inspect remit.
3. Test titles in a separate audience where appropriate: CEO, President, COO, CFO, Founder,
   Managing Director, EVP/SVP/VP, Head of Catalog, Head of Publishing, Head of Digital/Operations;
   for investment platforms, CIO, Managing Partner, Head of Acquisitions/Investments/Portfolio.
   “Head of” may map outside executive seniority. Do not assume arbitrary combinations of title
   and seniority filters are permitted.
4. Use classic/manual targeting where available and keep predictive/audience expansion off for
   the initial ICP test. Employee geography is separate from a company's headquarters. Choose
   profile language with the creative; worldwide geography does not mean worldwide language coverage.
5. Apply current customer exclusions if this is acquisition advertising. **CRM suppression has not been performed**. No company here is represented as a verified net-new prospect. Check the live CRM before activation; do not auto-import this list into Attio.

A practical starting creative split is catalog acquisition/portfolio operations, rights
administration/revenue recovery, and screen-music catalog exploitation. These are proposed message
themes derived from the evidence, not proven demand or performance claims.
